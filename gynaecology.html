Gynaecology & Maternity Care

Expert care for every phase of a woman's journey

At ARR Hospital, we provide specialized care for women's health with a strong focus on safety, comfort and personalized attention. From adolescence to pregnancy, childbirth and beyond, our gynaecology services are designed to support women through every stage of life with confidence and care.

Led by Dr. S. Rajeswari, MD (OG), our team combines advanced medical technology with a compassionate, patient-centered approach. We offer comprehensive services including antenatal care, safe normal and caesarean deliveries, infertility evaluation and management of menstrual and hormonal concerns. Our goal is to ensure the best possible outcomes for both mother and baby while creating a supportive and reassuring healthcare experience.

Our Services

Comprehensive Gynaecology Services

We offer a complete range of women's health services under one roof — from routine check-ups and antenatal care to advanced surgical procedures — all delivered with compassion and clinical precision.

Antenatal & Pregnancy Care

Regular monitoring, nutritional guidance, ultrasound scans, and complete antenatal support to ensure a safe and healthy pregnancy journey for mother and baby.

Normal & Caesarean Delivery

Expert-led normal and operative deliveries with 24/7 obstetric support, advanced labour monitoring and a fully equipped operation theatre for safe outcomes.

Infertility & Reproductive Health

Comprehensive infertility evaluation, hormonal assessments and personalised treatment plans to support couples on their path to parenthood with sensitivity and clinical care.

Menstrual Disorder Treatment

Diagnosis and treatment of irregular cycles, PCOS, endometriosis and other menstrual concerns using evidence-based medical and surgical approaches.

Menopausal Care

Compassionate support and individualised management for perimenopausal and menopausal symptoms, helping women maintain health and quality of life through this transition.

Family Planning Services

Confidential counselling and access to a full range of contraceptive options to help families plan for the future with confidence and informed decision-making.
