Child Care & Pediatrics

Nurturing healthy beginnings for every child

At ARR Hospital, we understand that children need more than just medical treatment — they need gentle, caring and age-appropriate healthcare. Our pediatric department is dedicated to providing comprehensive health services for newborns, infants, children and adolescents in a warm, child-friendly environment.

Under the expert guidance of Dr. Divakar, MD, DNB (Pediatrics), our team delivers evidence-based care using the latest diagnostic tools and treatment protocols. From routine vaccinations and growth monitoring to managing acute childhood illnesses and neonatal emergencies, we are committed to supporting your child's health at every stage of growth and development.

Our Services

Comprehensive Pediatric Services

We offer a full range of child healthcare services from birth through adolescence — from preventive care and vaccinations to specialised treatment — all delivered with warmth and clinical precision.

Newborn & Neonatal Care

Specialised care for newborns including routine health assessments, jaundice management, feeding support and monitoring of premature or low-birth-weight babies in our neonatal unit.

Vaccination & Immunisation

Complete vaccination schedules following national and international guidelines, protecting your child against preventable diseases with timely and safe immunisation programmes.

Growth & Development Monitoring

Regular tracking of your child's height, weight and developmental milestones to ensure healthy growth, early detection of concerns and timely intervention when needed.

Childhood Illness Management

Expert diagnosis and treatment of common childhood conditions including fever, respiratory infections, diarrhoea, allergies, asthma and skin disorders with gentle, effective care.

Nutritional Guidance

Personalised dietary advice and breastfeeding support to address nutritional deficiencies, promote healthy eating habits and ensure optimal growth during formative years.

Pediatric Emergency Care

Round-the-clock emergency pediatric services to handle acute childhood illnesses, high fevers, breathing difficulties, seizures and other urgent medical situations promptly and safely.
